# Primate

This isn't my model mind you, this is the [default monkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blender_(software)#Suzanne) that comes with blender. Many 3D apps come with something like this, a premade "complex" model. 3DS Max has a [teapot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utah_teapot), for example. But blender's is the best for just monkeying around with.
